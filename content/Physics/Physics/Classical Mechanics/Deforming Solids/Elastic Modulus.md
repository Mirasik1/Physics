The **elastic modulus** or **Young Modulus**, also known as the **elastic modulus**, is a fundamental property of materials that measures their stiffness or ability to resist deformation under stress. For a given material, the Young Modulus remains constant in the linear region of the stress-strain curve. ^e1ae19
