1. **The fluid is nonviscous.** In a nonviscous fluid, internal friction is neglected. An onject moving through the fluid experiences no viscous force.
2. **The flow is steady.** In steady (laminar) flow, the velocity of the fluid at each point remains constant.
3. **The fluid is incompressible.** The density of an imcompressible fluid is constant.
4. **The flow is irrational.** In irrational flow, the fluid has no angular momentum about any point.
