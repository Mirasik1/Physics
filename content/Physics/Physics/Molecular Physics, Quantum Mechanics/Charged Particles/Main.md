- The particles that have mass equal or greater than Proton mass are called Baryons. Consists of 3 Quarks or 3 Anti-Quarks. Charge is always whole number.
- The particles that have mass less than Proton mass are called Mesons. Consists of Quark and Anti-Quark. Charge is always whole number.
