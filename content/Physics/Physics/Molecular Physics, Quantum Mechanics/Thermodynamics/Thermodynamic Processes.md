Isothermal $\Delta T=0$  $W=Q$
Isobaric $\Delta P=0$  $W=P\Delta V$
Isochoric $\Delta V=0$ $W=0$
Adiabatic $\Delta Q=0$
An adiabatic process is one in which **no heat** is allowed to flow into or out of system. This situation can occur if the system is extremely **well insulated**, or the process happens **so quickly** that  heat - which flows slowly - has no time to flow in or out.
