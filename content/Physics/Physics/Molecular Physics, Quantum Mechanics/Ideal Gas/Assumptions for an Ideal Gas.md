The ideal gas model depends on the following assumptions:

- Collisions between gas particles and between particles and the walls of the container are perfectly **elastic**, meaning there is no net loss of kinetic energy.
- There are **no attractive or repulsive forces** between gas particles. They only interact during collisions.
- Gas particles **move randomly** in all directions, and their velocities vary.
- The average distance between molecules is **much larger** than the size of the molecules.
