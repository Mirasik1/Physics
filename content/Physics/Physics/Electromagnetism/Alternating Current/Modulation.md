Modulation is the variation of either **the amplitude** or **the frequency** of the carrier wave in synchrony with **displacement** of information signal.

| Feature                 | Amplitude Modulation (AM)                                            | Frequency Modulation (FM)                                                    |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Definition**          | Varies the amplitude of the carrier wave based on the message signal | Varies the frequency of the carrier wave based on the message signal         |
| **Carrier Property**    | Constant frequency, varying amplitude                                | Constant amplitude, varying frequency                                        |
| **Noise Resistance**    | More prone to noise (since noise affects amplitude)                  | Less prone to noise (frequency changes are less affected by amplitude noise) |
| **Power Efficiency**    | Less efficient (power wasted in sidebands and carrier)               | More efficient (constant amplitude allows better power utilization)          |
| **Sound Quality**       | Lower quality (susceptible to static and interference)               | Higher quality (less distortion, better signal-to-noise ratio)               |
| **Range & Propagation** | Long-range but can be affected by obstacles                          | Shorter range but provides better clarity                                    |
| **Usage**               | AM Radio (530 kHz – 1700 kHz), Aircraft communication                | FM Radio (88 MHz – 108 MHz), TV signals, Mobile communication                |
