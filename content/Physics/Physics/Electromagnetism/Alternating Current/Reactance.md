It describes the opposition that inductors and capacitors offer to the flow of AC current. Unlike resistance, which opposes both AC and DC, reactance depends on the **frequency** of the AC signal.

Phase Difference
- In an **inductor**, the current **lags** the voltage by $90\degree$.
- In a **capacitor**, the current **leads** the voltage by $90\degree$.
