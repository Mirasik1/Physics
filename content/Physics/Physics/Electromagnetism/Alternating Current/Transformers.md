A transformer is a device for increasing or decreasing an ac voltage. A transformer consists of two coils of wire known as the **primary** and **secondary** coils. The two coils can be interwoven (with insulated wire); or they can be linked by an **iron core** which is laminated to **minimize eddy-current losses**.
![[Transformer Equation]]

If the secondary coil contains more loops than the primary coil, we have **step-up transformer**.
If the primary coil contains more loops than the secondary coil, we have **step-down transformer**.
