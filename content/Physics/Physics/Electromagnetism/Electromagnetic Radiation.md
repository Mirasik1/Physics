Electromagnetic (EM) radiation is a type of wave that consists of oscillating electric and magnetic fields, which travel through space at the [[Speed of Light]].

| Wave Type            | Wavelength Range $\lambda$ | Frequency Range $f$   | Common Applications                 |
| -------------------- | -------------------------- | --------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| **Radio Waves**      | $>1m$                      | $<10^9Hz$             | Communication, broadcasting         |
| **Microwaves**       | $1mm – 1m$                 | $10^9−10^{12}Hz$      | Wi-Fi, radar, cooking               |
| **Infrared (IR)**    | $700nm – 1mm$              | $10^{12} - 10^{14}Hz$ | Remote controls, thermal imaging    |
| **Visible Light**    | $360−760nm$                | $10^{14} - 10^{15}Hz$ | Human vision                        |
| **Ultraviolet (UV)** | $100−400 nm$               | $10^{15} - 10^{16}Hz$ | Sterilization, sunburns             |
| **X-rays**           | $10^{−12}−10^{-8} m$       | $10^{16} - 10^{18}Hz$ | Medical imaging                     |
| **Gamma Rays**       | $<10^{−12}m$               | $>10^{18}Hz$          | Nuclear radiation, cancer treatment |
