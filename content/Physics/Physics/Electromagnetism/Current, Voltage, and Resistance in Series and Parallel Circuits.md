### 1. Series Circuits
In a series circuit, components are connected end-to-end, forming a single path for the current.
#### **Key Properties**:

- **Current ($I$)**:  
    The current is the same through all components.
    $I_{\text {total}}=I_1=I_2=I_3=\dots$

- **Voltage ($V$)**:  
    The total voltage across the circuit is the sum of the voltages across each component.
    $V_{\text{total}} = V_1 + V_2 + V_3 + \dots$

- **Resistance ($R$)**:  
    The total resistance is the sum of the individual resistances.
    $R_{\text{total}} = R_1 + R_2 + R_3 + \dots$

---
### 2. Parallel Circuits
In a parallel circuit, components are connected across the same two points, forming multiple paths for the current.
#### **Key Properties**:

- **Current ($I$)**:  
    The total current is the sum of the currents through each branch.
    $I_{\text{total}} = I_1 + I_2 + I_3 + \dots$

- **Voltage ($V$)**:  
    The voltage is the same across all components.  
    $V_{\text{total}} = V_1 = V_2 = V_3 = \dots$

- **Resistance ($R$)**:  
    The total resistance is given by the reciprocal sum of the individual resistances.    
    $\frac{1}{R_{\text{total}}} = \frac{1}{R_1} + \frac{1}{R_2} + \frac{1}{R_3} + \dots$
