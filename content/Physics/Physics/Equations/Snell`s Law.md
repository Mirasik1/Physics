$$\frac {sin\theta _1}{sin\theta _2} = \frac {v_1}{v_2} = \frac {n_2}{n_1}$$
- $\theta _1$​: Angle of incidence (angle between the incident wave and the normal to the boundary).
- $\theta_2$​: Angle of refraction (angle between the refracted wave and the normal to the boundary).
- $v_1$: Speed of the wave in the first medium.
- $v_2$​: Speed of the wave in the second medium.
- $n_1$​: Refractive index of the first medium.
- $n_2$​: Refractive index of the second medium.
