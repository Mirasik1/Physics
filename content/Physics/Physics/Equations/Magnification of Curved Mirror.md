$$m=\frac{h_i}{h_o}=-\frac{d_i}{d_o}$$
1.  Distances ($d_o$ and $d_i$​) are measured from the pole of the mirror.
	- Positive distances: In front of the mirror (real).
	- Negative distances: Behind the mirror (virtual).
2. Heights ($h_o = h_i$)
	- Positive: Above the principal axis.
	- Negative: Below the principal axis.
