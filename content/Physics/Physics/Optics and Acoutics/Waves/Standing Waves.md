#waves 
A standing wave occurs when incident and the reflected waves combine according to the principle of superposition and vibrate at the right freqeuncy.

| Travelling wave                                                      | Standing wave                                                  |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| All points of the medium will experience the same changing amplitude | Adjacent points will have different amplitude                  |
| Adjacent particles have different phase                              | All particles on the same side of the node have the same phase |

Harmonic frequency - is a wave with a frequency that is a positive integer multiple of the frequency of the original wave, known as fundamental frequency.
Fundamental frequency - is the lowest frequency in the harmonic series.
