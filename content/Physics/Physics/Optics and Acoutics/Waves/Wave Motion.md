The **wavelength** of a continuous sinusoidal wave is the distance between two successive crests/troughs.
The **frequency** is the number of wavelengths that pass a given point per unit time.
The **amplitude** of a wave is the maximum height of a crest, or depth of a trough, related to the normal (equillibrium) level.
