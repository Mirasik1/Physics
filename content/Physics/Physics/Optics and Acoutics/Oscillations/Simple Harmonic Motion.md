An oscillation in which the force is proportional to the displacement and is in te opposute direction.
Features that indicate that the motion is simple harmonic:
1. acceleration is proportional to displacement.
2. acceleration is opposite to displacement.
3. linear graph needs to through origin (0, 0).
4. the gradient of the graph is negative.
